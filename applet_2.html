Two apples and five bananas weigh 800 g, while four apples and nine bananas weigh 1,500 g. An unknown package weighs 1150 g and contains at least three apples and three bananas, with more apples than bananas.

Decode the 4-digit passcode: the first two digits are the total number of fruits × 3. The third digit is the number of apples, and the fourth is the number of bananas.
